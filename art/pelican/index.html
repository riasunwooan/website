Pelican Pocket

Community service · Designer

An illustrated mental health origami toolkit: six cards (support awareness, breathing exercises, a reset manual, progress, coping with failure, and sleep hygiene) that fold into a pocket-sized pelican. Designed as a steady, offline companion that is customizable and always there, it offers grounding strategies, reframing prompts, and reminders of available support.

Shaped together with student leaders, including prefects, RAs, peer mentors and ISAs, it is officially included in Loomis Chaffee’s Sophomore Seminars and used in Connecticut libraries.
