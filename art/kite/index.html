Kite Project

Community service · with Hana Foundations

This project was inspired by the idea of flying kites to send messages to those in the sky, and the fact that, especially in Korea, conversations about mental health and suicide are stigmatized. I wanted this project (the process of making a kite together, writing messages, and lastly, flying it together) to allow conversations to happen more naturally, just like the Pelican Pocket project.

I collaborated with a group, Hana Foundations, that I met at an exhibition with some of my pieces, to have a class with the kids. Below are some photos :)
