Kite Kit: Unfinished Conversations

Community service · with Hana Foundations

In Korea, people sometimes fly kites to send messages to those in the sky. At the same time, conversations about mental health and suicide are still deeply stigmatized there, and the people left behind often carry words they never got to say. I wanted to give those words somewhere to go.

The project turns that into something you do with your hands: making a kite together, writing messages on it, and letting it fly. Like Pelican Pocket, the goal is for hard conversations to happen more naturally, through making something rather than being asked to talk.

I partnered with Hana Foundations, a group I met at an exhibition of my work, to run a class where kids built and decorated their own kites. From there I painted my own bird kite covered in messages, and designed Unfinished Conversations, a kite kit “for words left unsaid,” with a step-by-step message guide, a postcard and everything needed to send a message on the wind.
