Who am I?

“Art is proof of care.”— Brian Eno, What Art Does: An Unfinished Theory

I explore my world and its hearts with a scientist’s curiosity and an artist’s imagination.

As long as I can remember, I've been keenly observant.

Perhaps it's the influence of my endlessly inquisitive dad or my artist mom.

And as long as I can remember, I've also loved to…

…draw, using all kinds of mediums.

…build, whether that's with legos,

books,

sand & mud,

or anything lying around.

Thing is, I also adored superheroes so much,

(yes, my first ever dream was to become a firefighter for the longest time)

that I became one every day:

  • a superhero that solves every mystery,
  • can gift happiness with stickers,
  • use bubble guns to cure sickness,
  • navigate the dark underground and get rid of sadness,
  • know every music and word and language in the CD and tell people,
  • and many, many more.

At one point, I thought I stopped doing this, for the sake of reality.

What I realized, though, is that art was what made this possible, both then & now. And art, to me, is anything that makes a new world.

  • I may not be able to cure cancer, but I can digest its pain with a paintbrush or light up a smile with a pop of color.
  • I may not be able to erase someone’s darkest day, but I can design something that helps them imagine tomorrow.
  • I may not be able to rewrite the rules of the human body, but I can engineer the material that helps it heal.
  • I may not be able to rewrite the history that overlooked women veterans, but I can turn their memories into pages that outlive us all.
  • I may not be able to time-travel and sit beside Grandma again, but I can write her back into a room, and somehow, make my Spanish teacher cry for someone she never met.

This is why I strive to be a superhero each day, because with art, everything I imagine is real.

And so, I call myself an “artist.”
