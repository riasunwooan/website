Volunteer Worker, Win-Some Finds Thrift Store

Sep 2026 – present · Local thrift store

Overview

Win-Some Finds is a local thrift store that donates everything it raises to organizations in need, steps in to help local people when disasters like fires strike, and offers a daytime workplace for adults with mental disabilities.

I help boost the store and its mission with whatever it needs, from its online presence to community events.

What I do

  • Create social media posts, and model clothing for them
  • Organize clothing drives
  • Design posters
  • Plan community events: currently a Thanksgiving tea & a Halloween trick-or-treat
