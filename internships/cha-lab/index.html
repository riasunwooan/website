Intern, Cha Lab: Youth Mental Health Research & Social Initiatives

Jan 2025 – present · Yale Child Study Center, Yale School of Medicine

About

The Cha Lab ↗ focuses on youth suicide risk and prevention. Led by Dr. Christine Cha through a partnership between the Yale Child Study Center and the Yale Center for Brain and Mind Health, the lab partners with teens, families, clinicians and youth-serving communities to discover new suicide-preventing insights and solutions. Its mission is to prevent suicide, alleviate internal distress, and foster the well-being of the next generation.

As an intern, I work on research and lead public initiatives. I have contributed to 3 research projects and presented at 2 conferences as the lab’s representative, as well as attending different events with the lab.

Research

Mapping the Internal Debate: Latent Profiles of Real-Time Dynamics in Desire to Live and Desire to Die in Adolescents
Co-author · Feb 2026 – Jun 2026
Using 14 days of real-time smartphone check-ins from 121 adolescents, we identified four profiles of how the desire to live and the desire to die rise and fall together, and found that a weaker desire to live, not only a stronger desire to die, can signal risk.
Pending publication in Archives of Suicide Research.

Continual Learning with Elastic Weight Consolidation for House Tree Person Test
First-author · May 2025 – Mar 2026
An AI framework that detects features in children’s House-Tree-Person drawings to support scalable mental health screening.

Initiatives

Cha Lab VOICE Youth Advisory Board

Co-Founder & Youth President

The VOICE Youth Advisory Board (Valued Opinions Informing Change & Evidence) is made up of high schoolers from the Tri-State area with a personal connection to suicidal thoughts and behaviors. Researchers bring their projects to the board, and members help guide research questions, methods, measures, outcomes and the dissemination of findings. I lead the board’s monthly discussions.

Faces covered at members’ request.

An Instant Depression Screening Method via Valence and Arousal Prediction from EEG & GSR
Research · Apr 2024 – Jan 2025
Mentors: Kobi Osei-Mensah, Science Faculty, The Loomis Chaffee School & Dr. Peter Na, Assistant Professor of Psychiatry, Yale University
An objective, instant depression screen that predicts emotional valence and arousal from brain (EEG) and skin (GSR) signals, for teens who may not recognize or report their own symptoms. “Suicide is not just the death of an individual, but a social murder.”
Published in Journal of Student Research, Aug 2025; presented at the Loomis Chaffee Science Symposium, May 2025.

Association of Social Determinants of Health with Incident Mental Health Outcomes in US Veterans
Research · Jul 2025 – May 2026
Mentor: Dr. Peter Na, Assistant Professor of Psychiatry, Yale University
In a 3-year study of US veterans, a heavier burden of adverse social determinants of health predicted new depression, anxiety, PTSD, suicidal ideation and substance use disorders.
Published in International Journal of Social Psychiatry, May 2026; poster presented at Medical Education Day at Yale, 2026.
