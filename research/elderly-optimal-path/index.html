Finding Optimal Path for the Elderly Considering the Costs Based on Skyline

Mar 2022 – Dec 2022 · North London Collegiate School Jeju

Mentor Mr. Kiran Mandhare, Computer Science Faculty, North London Collegiate School Jeju

Business Plan|Top 10 Finalist, NFTE World Series of Innovation ’22 ↗ Paper PDF ↗

Abstract

Background

In modern society, navigation systems enable human beings to find the optimal route quickly. Although it has been reported that Korea will be classified as a “super-aged” society by 2025 and it is the country with the highest elderly pedestrian death rate, there is no proper navigation system suited for the elderly. While the majority of navigation systems have decreasing time as their priority, the important thing for seniors is safety.

Methods

In this research, I studied the criteria and the factors that the navigation system should consider for the elderly when finding the safest path and defining score functions based on it. The brute force and greedy algorithms are introduced as solutions for this newly defined score function. By adopting the Skyline Query, the performance and utility of the algorithm are increased.

Results

The efficiency of the proposed algorithm is proven by the evaluation conducted on the synthetic dataset compared to the other algorithms implementing the system. Varying several parameters, the proposed system in this paper is proven to be proper to adopt in the real world.

Conclusion

The paper suggests two platform systems as future works based on the pattern recognition and CCH algorithm in data-collecting for more optimal path recommendation.
