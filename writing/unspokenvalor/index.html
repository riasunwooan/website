How it came together

  1. Jan 2025

    The idea

    While researching veterans’ mental health with Dr. Peter Na (see the research), I noticed that only about 10% of our dataset were women veterans. I wanted to hear their stories.

  2. Feb 2025

    Reaching out

    I reached out to women veteran organizations and got in touch with Gladys Silva-Perales, U.S. Army veteran and founder of She Served Too.

  3. Mar 2025

    Valor coins & the luncheon

    I made hand-glazed valor coins of my own design and joined the Honoring Our Woman Warriors luncheon to meet veterans and invite them to share their stories.

  4. Apr 2025

    Interviews

    I interviewed each of the veterans.

  5. May – Oct 2025

    Writing & illustrating

    We chose the kind of creative writing that best suited each story (a newspaper field note, letters, a biography, a radio broadcast, an audio diary, a curation guide and more). I designed the book cover, special illustrations and art for each story, gathered handwritten annotations, and spent hours formatting.

    The art and pages

  6. Nov 2025

    Bookmarks & the reveal

    I designed bookmarks with 3D-printed veteran coins and organized the book reveal (while rushing to publish!).

  7. Nov 15, 2025

    Published & revealed

    The book was published and revealed at the Rocky Hill Veterans Home (Connecticut Department of Veterans Affairs) to 100+ attendees, including Luke Bronin, 67th Mayor of Hartford, who also gave a speech. Unspoken Valor is archived at the Library of Congress, the Military Women’s Memorial, the Connecticut Historical Society and the Daughters of the American Revolution, and is offered at the Loomis Chaffee bookstore and 5 Connecticut libraries.

  8. Feb 2026

    Back on campus

    We brought the book, and the veterans, back to Loomis Chaffee for a Dinner & a Draft event.

  9. Now

    Giving back

    300+ copies sold, with all profits (about $8,000) donated to Backpacks For Life ↗, a nonprofit for homeless veterans.
