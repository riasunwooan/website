Unspoken Valor: Voices of Our Women Veterans

Nov 2025

Written by Ria An & Katie Pham

Illustrated by Ria An

To all women who served with strength and bravery,
To all whose hearts burn with unyielding courage,
And to all still seeking the valor that waits within.

300+ copies sold, with all profits (about $8,000) donated to Backpacks For Life ↗, a nonprofit for homeless veterans.

How it came together

  1. Jan 2025

    Where it started

    While working on veterans’ mental health research with Dr. Peter Na (see the research), something kept bothering me: only about 10% of our dataset were women. Behind that small number were real women with stories no one seemed to be asking about, and I wanted to hear them.

  2. Feb 2025

    Reaching out

    So I started reaching out to every women veteran organization I could find. That’s how I met Gladys Silva-Perales, a U.S. Army veteran and the founder of She Served Too, who became the heart of this whole project.

  3. Mar 2025

    Valor coins & the luncheon

    I didn’t want to show up empty-handed, so I designed and hand-glazed my own valor coins as small thank-you gifts. Then I joined the Honoring Our Woman Warriors luncheon, met so many incredible women, and asked if they’d trust us with their stories.

  4. Apr 2025

    Listening

    My team and I sat down with each veteran one by one. Some conversations were funny, some were heavy, and many of them were stories they had never told anyone before.

  5. May – Oct 2025

    Writing & illustrating

    No two stories were the same, so we didn’t want them to read the same either. One became a newspaper field note, another a collection of letters, a radio broadcast, an audio diary, a curation guide... whatever form felt truest to each woman. I designed the cover, drew the special illustrations and the art for every story, collected handwritten annotations, and spent more hours formatting than I’d like to admit.

    The art and pages

  6. Nov 2025

    Bookmarks & the reveal

    Every book deserved a keepsake, so I designed bookmarks with 3D-printed veteran coins, all while planning the book reveal and rushing to get the book published in time!

  7. Nov 15, 2025

    The book is real!

    Unspoken Valor was published and revealed at the Rocky Hill Veterans Home (Connecticut Department of Veterans Affairs) in front of 100+ people, including Luke Bronin, the 67th Mayor of Hartford, who gave a speech. Seeing the veterans hold their own stories in their hands is something I’ll never forget.

    The book is now archived at the Library of Congress, the Military Women’s Memorial, the Connecticut Historical Society and the Daughters of the American Revolution, and you can find it at the Loomis Chaffee bookstore and 5 Connecticut libraries.

  8. Feb 2026

    Bringing it home

    A few months later, we brought the book, and the veterans, back to Loomis Chaffee for a Dinner & a Draft, so our community could meet the women behind the pages.

  9. Now

    Giving back

    Over 300 copies sold, and every bit of profit (about $8,000 so far) has gone to Backpacks For Life ↗, a nonprofit supporting homeless veterans.
