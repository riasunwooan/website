Writing

Welcome to my writing archive! As much as I enjoy writing, I love sharing my work, as each piece, whether a memoir, essay, or poem, is a fraction of who I am and a careful sketch of my tangled thoughts.

Maybe someone will find themself in it, which is what I assume happens when people tell me they loved my piece with real tears in their eyes. And hopefully, their heart is a tiny bit warmer afterwards. For each piece, I included a short blurb. Enjoy!
